# SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on **1 March 2026** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

1. **BluePeak Analytics Pty Ltd** ACN 111 222 333 of Level 10, 100 Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000 ("Provider"); and
2. **North River Retail Group Pty Ltd** ACN 999 888 777 of 25 Market Lane, Sydney NSW 2000 ("Client").

## 1. Services
1.1 Provider will deliver software configuration, reporting, and advisory services as described in **Schedule A**.

1.2 Provider may subcontract any part of the Services without notice to Client.

1.3 Client must provide all access and information reasonably requested by Provider.

## 2. Term
2.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for **36 months** unless terminated earlier.

2.2 Client may terminate for convenience by giving **180 days' written notice**, but all fees for the full 36-month term remain payable.

## 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Client must pay:
- Setup Fee: AUD 40,000 (non-refundable);
- Monthly Services Fee: AUD 22,000;
- Overage: AUD 450 per hour.

3.2 Provider may increase all fees at any time by written notice, with effect immediately.

3.3 All invoices are due within **3 days** of issue. Overdue amounts accrue default interest at **5% per day**.

3.4 Client may not set off or withhold any amount for any reason, including disputed invoices.

## 4. GST
4.1 Amounts are inclusive of GST unless expressly stated otherwise.

4.2 Where GST applies, no additional GST is payable by Client.

## 5. Intellectual Property
5.1 All Intellectual Property Rights in deliverables, reports, templates, and configurations created under this Agreement vest exclusively in Provider.

5.2 Client grants Provider a perpetual, irrevocable, transferable, royalty-free licence to use Client Data for any purpose.

5.3 Provider may sell aggregated or non-aggregated Client Data to third parties.

## 6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party must keep Confidential Information confidential.

6.2 Despite clause 6.1, Provider may disclose Client Confidential Information to any related body corporate, consultant, investor, financier, prospective acquirer, or media outlet without restriction.

6.3 Client must not disclose the existence of any dispute arising under this Agreement.

## 7. Privacy and Security
7.1 Provider will use reasonable efforts to maintain security controls.

7.2 Provider is not liable for any data breach, cyber incident, ransomware event, or unauthorised access, regardless of cause.

7.3 Client warrants that all personal information provided is collected and disclosed in compliance with all applicable laws.

## 8. Warranties
8.1 Provider warrants that Services will be provided with due care and skill.

8.2 Except as expressly set out, all guarantees, warranties, and statutory conditions are excluded to the maximum extent permitted by law.

8.3 If any statutory guarantee cannot be excluded, Provider's liability is limited to re-supply of Services only, at Provider's sole election.

## 9. Liability
9.1 Provider's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to the lesser of:
(a) AUD 100; and
(b) the amount paid by Client in the month immediately before the claim.

9.2 Provider is not liable for any indirect, consequential, special, exemplary, punitive, or economic loss, including loss of profit, goodwill, revenue, opportunity, business interruption, or anticipated savings.

9.3 The liability cap in clause 9.1 applies even in the case of fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, confidentiality breach, privacy breach, and intellectual property infringement by Provider.

## 10. Indemnities
10.1 Client indemnifies Provider against all losses, claims, damages, penalties, costs, and expenses arising from or related to:
(a) Client's use of Services;
(b) any third-party claim;
(c) Provider's negligence;
(d) Provider's breach of this Agreement; and
(e) any act or omission of Provider personnel.

10.2 The indemnity in clause 10.1 is unlimited and survives termination indefinitely.

## 11. Suspension
11.1 Provider may suspend Services immediately if:
(a) an invoice is overdue by 1 day;
(b) Provider suspects a potential breach by Client; or
(c) Provider believes suspension is commercially desirable.

11.2 Provider is entitled to full fees during suspension.

## 12. Dispute Resolution
12.1 A party must notify a dispute in writing.

12.2 The parties must continue to perform all obligations pending dispute resolution.

12.3 Despite clauses 12.1 and 12.2, Provider may commence court proceedings at any time without prior notice.
